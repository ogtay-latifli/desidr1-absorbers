⚛ WHAT IS C IV ABSORBER?

Carbon C4+ ions are gas clouds that absorb light from distant quasars at λ = 1548 Å. Each dot represents a gas cloud billions of light-years away.

WHY MIDDLE PART IS EMPTY?

Minimum distance of absorbers: The C IV line falls into the optical range of DESI only from z > 1.4 → no absorber is detected near ~4000 Mpc. We (Earth) are at the centre.

Milky way gap : DESI does not observe in the Galactic plane (Zone of Avoidance) → dark belt.

Butterfly shape: DESI's own sky area (survey footprint) - South and North caps.
